Spirit & Entity Release

A method developed by Laura Whitworth, offered as part of Claire's holistic and spiritual wellbeing services.

Spirit & Entity Release is a deep healing practice intended for those who feel heavy in ways that talking therapy alone hasn't been able to lift — emotionally, energetically, or spiritually. The sessions are quiet, careful, and held with intention.

The premise behind this work is simple: when we carry unresolved trauma, fear, addiction, or persistent low emotion, our energetic field can become weakened. In that weakened state, outside energies and attachments can take hold — quietly shaping behaviour, mood, and even our sense of self in ways that aren't truly ours.

Soul Center Healing Hypnosis works at this energetic layer. Through the session, Claire helps release earthbound spirits, attachments, implants, AI interferences, and any other energies that no longer belong to you. The aim is to gently return you to a clean, balanced, and fully sovereign state.

Clients consistently describe feeling lighter, clearer, and more themselves afterwards — like a fog has lifted that they didn't always realise was there. Many notice meaningful shifts in their lives in the days and weeks that follow.

The Process

Understanding the work

What is Entity Release?

Trauma — especially trauma that wasn't fully processed at the time — can leave openings in our energetic boundary. Through those openings, energies that aren't ours can attach, slowly influencing how we feel, behave, and see ourselves.

Entity release is the careful work of recognising what doesn't belong to you and helping it leave. It might be earthbound spirits, energetic implants, attachments from past experiences, or other influences. Each one is approached with respect — for you and for what's being released.

Once that work is complete, the chakras are restored, and we move forward into the deeper journey of past lives and the Higher Self.

Past Life Regression

Sometimes the things we struggle with most in this lifetime have roots that stretch much further back. Past life regression is a gentle journey into another life — one that may still be quietly shaping who you are today.

It's not about proving or disproving past lives. It's about giving the mind a frame to understand patterns and pain that haven't made sense any other way — and finally letting them go.

Higher Self

Beyond the everyday mind sits something quieter and wiser — what many traditions call the Higher Self. The part of you that already knows.

In this stage of the session, we open a connection with that aspect of you. Questions can be asked, clarity received, and any additional healing the body or spirit needs can be carried out — guided entirely by you.
